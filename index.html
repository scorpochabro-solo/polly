Process

From research to feedback

The workflow starts with the audience and product, then moves into packaging, conversion platforms, traffic launch, optimization and result analysis.

  1. 01

    Persona map

    Segment the audience and define key communication points.

  2. 02

    Product map

    Clarify the offer, service structure and sales geography.

  3. 03

    Competitor audit

    Review direct and indirect competitors through packaging and offer logic.

  4. 04

    Service packaging

    Build the visible offer and the elements needed for interaction.

  5. 05

    Conversion platforms

    Prepare website, social media and messengers to receive traffic.

  6. 06

    Traffic launch

    Send traffic to prepared platforms and test working combinations.

  7. 07

    Optimization

    Improve campaigns, creatives and scenarios based on audience response.

  8. 08

    Result analysis

    Evaluate leads, measurable indicators and project feedback.
