FAQ

Answers before you ask

The questions that come up most before starting a project.

Do you work with my country and remotely?

Yes. Everything runs remotely with the same structure for any market — private blogs, businesses and B2B worldwide.

Can I start with just one thing — only ads, or only a website?

Yes. The work can start from a single piece — audit, website, SMM, creatives, paid ads or analytics — and connect into a full system later.

How do you report results?

With real numbers from the ad platforms and Yandex Metrica when access is available. No invented metrics — only what's genuinely measurable for your project.

How long until we launch and see results?

After research and packaging, traffic is launched and tested first, then optimized by audience response. Timing depends on scope and the platforms involved.

What do you need from me to start?

Context on your offer and audience, access to the relevant accounts and platforms, and the goal you want to reach.

How is pricing decided?

By scope. Packages start “from” a base and are tailored to the project — the audit is the simplest way to get an exact figure.
