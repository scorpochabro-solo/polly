Process · 01—08

Eight moves from clarity to growth.

A connected story from understanding the product to reading the real result — not a stack of disconnected deliverables.

  1. 01

    Persona map

    Segment the audience and find the communication points that matter.

  2. 02

    Product map

    Clarify the offer, service structure and sales geography.

  3. 03

    Competitor audit

    Review direct and indirect competitors, packaging and offer logic.

  4. 04

    Service packaging

    Make the offer and interaction points clear and visible.

  5. 05

    Conversion platforms

    Prepare the website, social media and messengers.

  6. 06

    Traffic launch

    Run traffic and test channel–creative combinations.

  7. 07

    Optimization

    Improve campaigns, creatives and scenarios from audience response.

  8. 08

    Result analysis

    Read leads, measurable indicators and project feedback.
