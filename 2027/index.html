Photo stories.

Five shoots from Polina Lazareva’s portfolio. Look closer at a piece of jewellery, an open notebook, a plate or a room.

Jewellery

Lumare Dolce

A pair of drop earrings hangs from a stem instead of a display stand. Across the series, leaves, oyster shells and cut wood set off the colours of the stones without competing with the fine chains.

Stationery

A planner in hand

The covers are only half the story. A hand poised above a lined page shows the space inside; a paired portrait picks up the red and charcoal of the covers in the clothes behind them.

Food photography

At the breakfast table

The overhead frame brings croissants, strawberries and sliced pear into one composition. Turning the camera upright changes the emphasis: first to the basket and flowers, then to the cut fruit and folds of wrapping paper.

Interior photography

A room, then a detail

The wide shot establishes the shelving, seating and lines of light. Moving along the sofa and in towards the yellow magazine rack gives a sense of how the space fits together, not just what is in it.

Artisan food

The cheesemaker’s board

One frame uses a pair of hands to give the serving a sense of scale. The next moves close to a cut wheel, where the speckled interior becomes visible. An overhead arrangement brings the different cheeses back together.

Our process

From brief
to launch.

  1. Understand the audience

    Map customer needs and the product. Review competitors to see which questions the offer needs to answer.

  2. Shape the offer

    Clarify the service and prepare the website, social profile or messaging channel where enquiries will arrive.

  3. Launch the campaign

    Run paid traffic, compare creatives and adjust the campaign as people respond.

  4. Read the results

    Review traffic, enquiries and customer feedback. Use what we learn to plan the next round.
